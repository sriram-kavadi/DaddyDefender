Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Threat Check is an AI-powered digital threat detection and trust platform that checks links and messages for signs of phishing, scams, and social engineering. This page explains how data is handled across our operating modes.

Operating Modes & Data Handling

Threat Check supports two operating architectures:

  • Client-Side / Offline Mode: If the backend API is unreachable or when using static hosting, analysis runs entirely inside your browser via local JavaScript heuristics. Nothing you paste is uploaded to any server.
  • Connected Backend & Threat Intelligence Mode: When connected to the Threat Check backend server, URLs and messages may be checked against threat intelligence repositories (such as URLhaus and VirusTotal). Lookups only submit the URL or domain being checked, never personal identifiers.

Data Storage & Persistence

  • Browser Storage: The analyzer saves the first 80 characters of recent checks locally in your browser so you can view past scans. You can clear this anytime using the "Clear history" button.
  • Backend Database: When the backend server is running, completed scans are persisted in a local SQLite database for auditing and statistics. Only a redacted preview (up to 80 characters), risk metrics, and findings are retained. You can delete individual scans directly from the Server Scans tab.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell or monetize your data.
  • We do not track you with third-party advertising or commercial tracking cookies.
  • We do not store plain-text passwords or secret credentials submitted in messages.

Hosting & Third Parties

When threat intelligence lookups are enabled on the backend server, API requests to external threat intelligence providers (such as URLhaus or VirusTotal) are performed server-side without exposing API keys to the browser.

Questions

For questions about this policy or the application architecture, open an issue in the project repository.
